Our Story

About Us

At Films by Manish, we believe weddings are not just events — they are a collection of emotions, relationships, and moments that deserve to be remembered just as they were felt. Our approach is rooted in capturing real, unscripted moments and turning them into timeless stories that you can relive for years to come.

We focus on documenting weddings naturally, without forcing or staging moments. From the big celebrations to the quiet in-between emotions, we aim to capture everything with honesty and attention to detail. Every wedding is unique, and we strive to reflect that individuality through our work.

Over the years, we've had the privilege of working with couples across India and beyond, especially those who travel back home to celebrate their weddings. With a dedicated team that shares the same vision, we ensure every couple feels comfortable and every memory is preserved beautifully and authentically.
